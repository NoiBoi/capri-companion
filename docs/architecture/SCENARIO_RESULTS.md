The clock advances 48 hours with 2,280 active minute ticks, seven synthetic native executions, three restart boundaries and zero model calls. Final SQLite: 311,296 bytes. Receipts use a synthetic negative-monitor position; actual Windows placement/restart tests provide separate native evidence. This is not a live two-day experiment.

## Qualitative findings, including failures

- Concrete technical views developed when reversing load and rough finish were introduced. No actual CAD inspection was claimed. The song opinion and its reasons survived disagreement and next-day recall. Both runs recalled the rough-finish constraint after restart and compaction.
- Focus, unchanged video/deadline evidence, gaming/call, location uncertainty, source-injection and explicitly requested silent company produced raw silence. Repeated topic callbacks after criticism were suppressed; generated phrase retirement was not used as a global ban.
- The second sequence produced a specific subscription/ownership opinion on fresh title evidence. Its next correction incorrectly admitted having misread the creator's position, although the prior output had stated its own view. That is an attribution failure, not proof that the original observation was wrong.
- Both sequences still credited themselves for staying quiet when work resumed (turn 13). The second softened a song view and then overaccepted the criticism of that softening. Repeated Fair/opening concessions and a familiar hypothetical wandering motif remain weaknesses. The guide discourages these; it does not guarantee model compliance.
- Both generic optional-event dialogues were silent when the deferred email became available (turn 24). After the actual execution-lane audit and improved deferred-request direction, a separate one-attempt production BehaviorCore evaluation made zero model calls while gaming, then one Luna call and one synthetic confirmed delivery: Your advisor’s waiting on a quick reply: does tomorrow at 10 work for the lab review? It did not claim the email had been read or a meeting attended. Its actual candidate transitioned to delivered. This targeted success does not erase the two broader failures.
- The 1.0 review used the same broad fictional scenario and already showed useful opinions and technical continuity. This release has concrete authority/provenance/outcome improvements; the small, non-blinded conversations do not establish a general subjective superiority or lasting immersion over 1.0.

The actual packaged native-session restart test is recorded with the full Windows results in FINAL_REPORT.md. Real TTS, arbitrary private-service data, an attended meeting, actual gym activity and 48 hours of uninterrupted live companionship were not established by these fixtures.
