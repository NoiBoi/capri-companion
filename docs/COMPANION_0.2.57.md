# Capri 0.2.57: lasting voice and situated company

`docs/DIALOGUE_GUIDE.md` is the cumulative authoring source. Its marked runtime
section is compiled into both direct conversation and spontaneous decisions.
`AGENTS.md` tells future editors to preserve its preference ledger, reconcile
explicit corrections, and keep speech and movement changes together. Rebuild
and restart after editing the guide. Examples are original illustrations, not
fixed outputs or claimed show quotations.

The guide strengthens Capri's Night City voice, independent tastes, self
reference, and grounded continuity. Identified songs can draw on knowledge
without inventing live hearing. Her source, appearance, settings and movement
can prompt opinions when the actual context identifies them. Earlier memory,
project and conversation continuity remains in place; no relationship scores
or new unlimited background inference was introduced.

YouTube comments prioritize the creator, then title/topic. A bounded Windows
browser-media lookup supplies a channel only when its video title matches the
target window. Missing or ambiguous metadata falls back to the window title,
without inventing a creator. These comments do not require screenshots. A
settled new video can prompt a deliberate visit; an unchanged title/channel
does not repeatedly earn the same unsolicited remark.

New windows can attract a prompt visit despite an older exploration rest.
Visits may tour the exposed window, teleport directly to a top perch, or rest
on a supported lower edge. Lower-edge attachment follows the window and loses
support if it closes or becomes unsafe. Cursor visits briefly follow meaningful
pointer movement, keep clear of the click point, and return home. Quiet, sleep,
focus, interactions and geometry can cancel these actions.

Actual late-hour or low-battery cues can draw her toward the lower-right taskbar
area of the active work monitor. This targets the region rather than reading
the exact clock or battery icon rectangle. Battery behavior requires a real
battery report; desktop PCs do not acquire an invented battery. Nearby pointer
movement or a fresh observed view can motivate a small in-window adjustment.
Time limits bound these behaviors but do not themselves supply a movement cause.

The authored interaction bank adds many instant pat, poke, pickup, putdown and
same-window relocation reactions. Each of those banks has at least 20 opening
variants, alongside existing combinations. Contact often produces an immediate
line and then a generated continuation. Pats have an 80% continuation candidate
rate after an immediate line, subject to context, rapid-touch restraint, model
availability and the shared request allowance. A continuation may choose quiet.

Spotify track changes use one hidden Windows media helper, sampling metadata
every 12 seconds, followed by a short stabilization delay. Rapid skips, repeated
tracks and quiet conditions are filtered. No audio or microphone is captured,
and no Spotify login is used. This supports Spotify media sessions, not every
possible player. A local native probe measured about 96 MB helper working set
and 0.016 CPU seconds over a 12.5 second interval, rather than zero resource use.

Idle and sleep returns now have immediate authored text greetings independent
of TTS and model connectivity. Lounge/stay modes and an unavailable teleport
no longer silently swallow the greeting. User quiet controls still apply.

Opening Capri's own settings or developer page now triggers a self-related
authored reaction, with a possible generated continuation of the current
exchange. Actual panel navigation supplies the cue, including entry from the
tray. Settings pages share a five-minute repeat guard, developer has its own,
and quiet/composer controls apply. Opening a page does not imply any setting
changed or any developer scenario started.

Long rests remain intentional: completed outings can rest for 8 to 12 minutes,
and some visits finish hovering rather than perching. The foreground app is
not the same as Capri's chosen location. A visit can remain quiet; movement
does not force speech. Fresh-window and stable-new-video cues can interrupt
ordinary rest when eligible.

The existing activity controls remain: Lively admits 24 ambient requests per
10 minutes, with 32 ambient plus memory attempts combined and a 15 second
unsolicited spacing floor. Provider cooldowns remain authoritative.

## Validation record

Source validation passed type checking, 523 unit tests, and 31 targeted app
checks. Earlier failures exposed startup geometry and inspection-arrival timing;
the related paths and checks were corrected. Packaged validation and deployment
results are recorded with the release outputs.

Packaged validation passed 44 selected checks across eight suites (three
optional checks skipped), followed by seven focused placement/return/game
regressions. After the settings/developer addition, a focused source check
passed and the rebuilt package passed three final panel/return checks. The
installed executable reported 0.2.57, connected to Codex, and became ready in
1,154 ms. All 40 approved memories loaded; memory, history and encrypted
credentials were byte-for-byte unchanged at the restart verification. The
previous application and a stopped-profile backup are retained for rollback.

The first live sample used the configured gpt-6-luna model. Its music opinion
was knowledgeable but fairly plain. Its self-code replies had a clearer view:
"Fix the bugs, sure, but I'm keeping the option to move." The first live pat
continuation was cancelled while the harness still depended on the actual
foreground desktop. That result remains in the saved sample, alongside any
later controlled-context check. The controlled packaged check did deliver both
the instant pat line and a generated continuation retaining the objection to
being made stationary. Prompt inclusion and mocked app checks prove
wiring; a few live samples do not establish consistently show-perfect dialogue.
